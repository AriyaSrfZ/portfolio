# Ariya Sarrafzadeh — Brand Identity & Design System

## 1. Positioning & Tone
- **Title**: Principal Systems Architect & Technical Product Leader
- **Core Tagline**: *"Systems that hold under pressure."*
- **Archetype**: Senior engineering authority operating in mission-critical infrastructure (banking switches, high-concurrency telecom hubs, ACID distributed ledgers).
- **Tone Balance**: 65% Industrial Warmth (editorial paper, aged porcelain, warm ochre) : 35% Cold Austerity (deep carbon, slate basalt, architectural patina).
- **Zero AI-Slop Rule**: Strictly forbid generic cyberpunk neon cyan (`#06b6d4`, `#38bdf8`), floating telemetry particles, fake glass bubble cards, and conversational filler.

## 2. Canonical Color Tokens
- **Backgrounds**:
  - Deep Carbon: `#171C1A` (`var(--bg-primary)`)
  - Carbon: `#202725` (`var(--surface)`)
  - Basalt: `#303936` (`var(--surface-card)`)
  - Basalt Border: `#3A4441` / `#46504B` (`var(--border)`)
- **Light Surfaces**:
  - Paper: `#E9E5DC`
  - Porcelain: `#F3F0E8`
- **Accents**:
  - Canonical Patina: `#527C78` (`var(--accent-primary)`)
  - Patina Light: `#8FB4AA` (`var(--accent-light)`)
  - Patina Sage: `#6E9792`
