# Visual & Engineering Architecture Directive

## Aesthetic Rule: "Anti-Slop & Human-Crafted"
- NO generic AI purple gradients (#7C3AED, #8B5CF6, etc.).
- Dominant palette: Deep rich warm-dark surfaces (`#141210`, `#0C0A09`, `#18181B`).
- Glassmorphism & Elevation: Subtle backdrop blur (`bg-[#141210]/40 backdrop-blur-md border border-white/10`).
- Micro-interactions: Deliberate, tactile feedback (`transition-all duration-500 hover:-translate-y-1 hover:shadow-2xl hover:border-white/20`).
- Typography: High contrast, crisp tracking, editorial layout (Bento grid, clean metrics, product leadership focus).
- Enforce: Strictly follow `impeccable` rules and `taste-skill` standards for all component updates.
