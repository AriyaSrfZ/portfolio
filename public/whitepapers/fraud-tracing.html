01 // Defense Strategy

Multi-Tiered Perimeter Defense

Static heuristic rules introduce severe operational bottlenecks. Malicious entities adapt to velocity checks and country blacklists, while legitimate buyers face false rejections.

Modern platforms deploy multi-layered perimeter architectures: deterministic filtering at ingress, edge telemetry collection, ML behavioral scoring, and post-transaction graph analysis.

MATHEMATICAL STANDARD: Chargeback Ratio = (Chargebacks Received in Month N / Total Transactions in Month N-1) * 100
02 // Detection Engines

Heuristics vs. Behavioral ML Models

Heuristic Rule Filtering

Evaluates rigid static parameters, including velocity caps per card token, geographic IP mismatches, and compromised BIN tables. Executes under 5ms but yields elevated false-positive rates on legitimate traffic surges.

Behavioral ML Scoring

Evaluates high-dimensional telemetry vectors, including typing cadence, device orientation entropy, and entity cluster graphs. Computes risk vectors between 0.00 and 1.00 within a 25ms execution SLA.

3D-Secure 2.0 Shift

Transmits device telemetry payloads directly to issuer Access Control Servers. Frictionless approval occurs on low-risk transactions while shifting financial dispute liability from the merchant to the card issuer.
