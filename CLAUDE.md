## 03 / ANTI-AI-SLOP DIRECTIVES
1. **NO Generic Gradients**: Ban all `from-purple-500 to-indigo-600` or neon glow effects.
2. **NO Cliché Hero Copy**: Ban phrases like *"Empowering the future"*, *"Passionate innovator"*, or *"Unlocking potential"*.
3. **NO Rounded Pill Badges Everywhere**: Use crisp, structured, data-dense badges with subtle borders (`#343C38`).
4. **NO Gratuitous Emojis**: Use structured monospace labels (`[METRIC]`, `[ARCH]`, `[TELECOM]`).
5. **High Typography Contrast**: Monospace numbers and code tokens with high contrast.
